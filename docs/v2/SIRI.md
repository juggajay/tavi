# Siri and Tavi

## What you need first

The PC must be on and reachable through your own private HTTPS address. Pair your phone first and check Tavi in its browser. Shortcuts use a separate key made on the PC; keep that key private and revoke it if the phone is lost. Never paste it into a conversation or public issue. The installation guide must supply your exact address and reviewed Shortcut setup.

## Pick a name

Use a clear shortcut name such as Tell Mate or Open Mate. Replace Mate with the bot's chosen name. Very short names and names like Siri can be misheard.

## 1. Tell Mate

A text shortcut sends your words through the paired Shortcut entry point to your chosen bot. Its request is a message, never an approval to send or change something outside. Try it with a harmless question first. Do not put private words in the shortcut's name.

## 2. Open Mate

An open shortcut opens your own paired Tavi phone address. Save that address yourself; an AI helper must not guess a private network name or copy another user's address.

## 3. Actions still need you

Read any draft or proposed change in Tavi. Approve it by the app's own control when you want it done. Speaking to Siri cannot approve a send. A website signed in on a bot screen has different risks; see the safety guide.

## 4. Share with Mate

When configured by the installation guide, the share shortcut passes chosen text or a file to Tavi. Check what you selected before sharing. It becomes information for the bot, not permission to publish it or add it to a web query.

## 5. Using voice away from the PC

The PC must remain awake and your phone must reach it privately. Check microphone permissions and audio output. Do not interact with a screen while driving. Wait until safely stopped before reviewing drafts or results.

## 6. Remove access

On the PC use the shortcut unpair command listed in AGENTS.md to revoke its key. Remove the corresponding Shortcuts on the phone and revoke phone pairing if appropriate. Removing a shortcut alone does not revoke a copied key.

## If something goes wrong

Try the paired phone page first, then a short typed message. Check PC power, private HTTPS, pairing and subscription state. Recreate a revoked key through local setup without printing it into logs. The general troubleshooting guide explains the guard and server logs.
