Connections

What Tavi can use, and what it's allowed to do with each. Nothing is ever sent or changed until you tap Approve on your phone.

Read lets Tavi look things up. Write lets it make changes when you ask, like sending an email or adding a calendar event, but every single one waits for you to tap Approve on your phone. Saving a draft doesn't need a tap.
These come through your Claude account: click Connect, sign in on claude.ai, then come back here and press Check again.

Websites its own browser on this PC

For a site you list here, you log in yourself in the window that opens. Tavi never sees, types or stores your password: the site just keeps that browser signed in, like your own browser does, in a profile on this PC that Edge encrypts with your Windows account. If it offers to save your password, say no. Remove logs it out of that site; Forget all logins wipes every one. Jobs for a saved site can only use that site. For any other site it uses a fresh browser with no logins. Either way, it asks your go-ahead on the phone, with a screenshot, before anything is ordered, paid, booked, sent or submitted.
